### About the Project

MineMirror AI is a real-time **Physics-Informed Digital Twin** platform designed for underground and open-pit mining operations.

It creates a living virtual replica of an entire mine that continuously synchronises with IoT sensors, equipment telemetry and geological models.  

At its core, **Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)** embed rock-mechanics and machine-physics equations into the predictive engine — enabling accurate early warnings of rock bursts, predictive maintenance and occupational health management even when historical failure data is scarce.

---

### Key Features

- Predictive Safety (rock-burst early warning with 2–12 hour lead time)
- Equipment Digital Twins & Remaining Useful Life prediction
- Occupational Health Layer (dust, heat, noise, vibration)
- Closed-loop safety interlocks (<200 ms latency)
- Process & Sustainability optimisation
- Hybrid Edge-Cloud architecture
