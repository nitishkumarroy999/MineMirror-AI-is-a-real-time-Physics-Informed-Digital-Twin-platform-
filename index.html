How a Digital Twin Works in Mining

A physics-informed digital twin is not just a 3D model. It is a living system that mirrors the real mine in real time.

1

Data Acquisition

Sensors, SCADA, equipment telemetry, geological models, drone surveys and existing control systems continuously feed live data into the twin.

2

Physics + AI Fusion

First-principles models (rock mechanics, fluid flow, thermodynamics) are combined with machine-learning patterns so the twin remains accurate even with sparse failure data.

3

Synchronised Virtual Mine

A high-fidelity 3D representation stays locked to real conditions — geometry, stress, equipment health, environment and production flow.

4

Prediction & Action

The twin runs forward simulations, detects anomalies, estimates remaining useful life and delivers clear alerts and recommended actions to operators and planners.
